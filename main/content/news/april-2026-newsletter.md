The library van will be visiting on Wednesday 29th April and Wednesday 27th May.

to loan another book.

The week commencing the 27th April, you will receive a copy of your child’s
class curriculum overview of planned learning for the term for children in P1-3,
P3-6 and P6/7.

## P3-6 Outdoor Learning

Mrs Lannagan’s class with be having weekly trips to the Castle grounds,
starting on Friday 1st May. Please ensure that pupils have snack and a water
bottle. Pupils will be leaving at 9am sharp and will be back in school before
11.30am.

## School closure – reminder

A reminder that the school will be closed on Monday 4th May for the May Day
Bank Holiday. Staff will resume on Tuesday 5th May for an In-Service day but
school will remain closed to pupils. School is open to all on Wednesday 6th May.

## P7 Residential

Primary 7 pupils are attending the cluster residential from Wednesday 6th May to
Friday 8th May with Mrs MacLeod. Information on the trip including kit lists was
provided to parents / carers in March. Please contact the school office if you
would like an additional copy. A further letter will be issued next week with
additional information.

## P6 Jim Clarke Museum

Following the very successful Goblin Car Project, P6 children will visit the
Jim Clark Museum on Wednesday 13th May to learn more about motorsport and
engineering. Permissions and further information will be communicated to parents
/ carers.

## P5-7 Rugby

James Thompson the Rugby Development Officer has begun teaching pupils for PE
sessions, to prepare the P7’s for the Primary 7 Rugby festival on Thursday
14th May, and to give the P4 pupils an introduction to rugby.

## Walk to School Week

This will be held during the week of 18th May. Our Junior Road Safety Officer
from Primary 7 will be sharing information with the children about this.

It is lovely to see so many children travelling to school in the village on their
scooters and bikes, however they should wear a helmet for their own safety and
the safety of drivers on the roads.

## P5 Countryside Day

On Tuesday 19th May Primary 5 pupils will travel to Springwood Park in Kelso
with a member of staff, to take part in Countryside Day. Pupils will require a
packed lunch and snack. Please wear school uniform. The bus will return in time
for normal transport arrangements home.

## Parent Consultations

Parent consultations are being held on Wednesday 20th May between 5:00pm and
7:00pm and Thursday 21st May between 3:30pm-5:00pm. Parents Evening Booking
System (PEBS) information will be communicated to nearer the time to allow
everyone an opportunity to book an appointment with class teachers and Miss
Morton, support for learning teacher. The booking system will be live from
8.30am on Friday 1st May and will close on Friday 15th May. Please note that
amendments requested after his date cannot be accommodated. A link will be
emailed to all parents / carers with more information.

## Sponsored Walk

The PFA have planned a sponsored walk for the children to take part in on
Monday 25th May around the village/castle grounds. All money raised will be
gratefully received and will go towards playground improvements. Please look for
a sponsorship form which will be handed out shortly.

## Attendance

Please do phone the school if your child will be absent from school. The school
office hours are 8.35am to 3.10pm, Monday to Thursday and 8.35am to 12.30 on a
Friday. Mrs McAskill will send a group call message if a child is absent and we
have not heard why.

## Parent-Pay

Please continue to make meal choices for your child before 8.00am on the day you
wish the booking to be taken.

## Changes in pick up

Please contact the school before 2.30pm if you are changing your child’s pick
up arrangements at the end of the day. It is very important that we know of have
changes so that class teachers can be informed. You can contact us by telephone,
email or Groupcall.

## Water bottle and Snacks

Please ensure your child is sent to school with a filled water bottle and with
a snack for break-time. We also have a fruit fix in the afternoons, pupils are
encouraged to bring fruit in for an afternoon snack.

## Breakfast Club

Breakfast continues to operate daily. Breakfast club is open from 08.15am -
08.30am every day and costs 50p. Please remember to book on ParentPay if you are
using breakfast club to ensure there are no overdue payments. If you feel that
you could volunteer for 45 minutes on any morning, please contact the school
office for further information.
