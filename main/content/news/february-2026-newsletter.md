## ParentPay

Please remember to make bookings in advance for your children’s lunches, if
bookings are not made by 8.00am, children will be offered a meal, if they have
not brought a packed lunch. It is important to book in advance so the kitchen
has enough stock for each day.

## Attendance

Please do phone or message via EduSpot, if your child will be absent from
school. The school office hours are 8.35am to 3.10pm, Monday to Thursday and
8.35am to 12.30 on a Friday. Mrs McAskill will send a group call message if a
child is absent and we have not heard why.

## Showbie

Showbie is a secure, online learning platform where teachers and students
can collaborate, celebrate success, empower learners, share content, and use
educational apps to supplement their learning from their class teacher. Please
remember to create an account and request to join the class noticeboards.
Showbie class codes can be found on the Curriculum Overview which was sent to
all parents to weeks ago. Curriculum overviews are also available to view on
Showbie.

## P5-7 Cross Country

LiveBorders have organised for some of the P5-7 to take part in cross country,
which was due to take place in Eyemouth Primary School on Thursday 5th February
from 10am until 12 noon. Please note this date has now changed due to weather
and slippery surfaces. It has now been rescheduled for Thursday 26th February.
If your child has chosen to take part, you will have received a letter from
school already. Transport will be provided, and children will be back in school
for lunchtime. Children should come dressed in warm outdoor P.E. clothes with a
water bottle and snack. Permission forms are to be completed on Parents Portal.

## Generation Science

On Wednesday 25th February we are welcoming back Generation Science to school.
This year all of P5 – P7 will be taking part in a workshop called “Electric
Explorers” – We will learn to generate green electricity from our own
movement, then race mini-robotic creatures using our own power! Many thanks to
our PFA for funding this fantastic event for our second level learners.
