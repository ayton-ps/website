## PFA Sponsored Walk

The Sponsored Walk has been a huge success and raised over £1000! A huge thank
you to all involved and a special thank you to Darren and Linda Hall from Ayton
Mini Market for the very refreshing ice lollies at the halfway point.

## Whole School Trip – Northumberland Zoo

We are delighted to share that on Monday 29th June we will all be heading off to
Northumberland Zoo for a whole school end of term trip. A huge thank you to our
PFA who have fully funded this brilliant opportunity for our learners at Ayton.
We will be leaving at 9am and returning at 3pm. All classes will be taking part
in an interactive workshop, a tour of the zoo and play opportunities on site.
All children will need a packed lunch. A letter and online permission form will
be issued shortly to all families.

## PFA Meeting

The next PFA meeting will be held on the 2nd of June at 8pm online. All
parents/carers are welcome to attend.

## P6 Inspire Learning Day – Springwood Park, Kelso

On Wednesday 3rd of June P6 pupils will be joining other schools from around
the Scottish Borders to take part in Inspire Learning Day at Springwood Park in
Kelso. Pupils will be leaving school at 8:55am, sharp. Pupils will need a packed
lunch, snack, water bottle and weather appropriate clothes. The consent form is
available on ParentsPortal and letters have already gone home with P6 pupils.

## P7 Parent Information Session at Eyemouth High School

Parents / carers of Primary 7 pupils are invited to the information session at
Eyemouth High School on Thursday 4th June, 5.30-6pm.

## Primary 6 Priory Live

On Tuesday 9th June, Primary 6 pupils will be attending Coldingham Priory
to participate in their ‘Priory Live’ event with other schools from our
cluster and Mrs Nottingham. Please bring a packed lunch, snack and water bottle.
Children should wear their school uniform and sunscreen as needed. The bus
will return in time for normal transport arrangements home. Please use the new
Parents Portal app to consent to this trip.

## P7 to S1 School Transition

Primary 7 children have been enjoying transition days with their peers within
our cluster during May and will attend Eyemouth High School on Tuesday 9th and
Wednesday 10th June for the two formal transition days.

Children are expected to travel to Eyemouth High School on Tuesday 9th and
Wednesday 10th June by the transport that they are to travel to Eyemouth High
School on in August using the Young Scots/NEC card which they have received.
Children will follow a timetable for the two days.

## School Bake Sale

Our Pupil Council have decided to hold a Bake Sale again this year, to raise
funds for Outdoor Learning resources. This will be held on Friday 12th June. All
pupils in the school will be given the opportunity to buy a cake from our stall.
They can bring in some small loose change that day, which their teacher can
look after until the event. We hope this will be a fun event for our children
to enjoy, whilst also raising much needed funds to enhance their learning
experiences.

## Attendance

Please ensure your child arrives at the start of the school day on time, as we
still have a few children arriving after the bell at 8.45am.

Please do phone the school if your child is absent from school. You can also
use the EduSpot app to notify the school. The school office hours are 8.30am to
3.10pm, Monday to Thursday and 8.30am to 12.30 on a Friday. Mrs McAskill will
send a group call message if a child is absent and we have not been given a
reason.

## Parent-Pay

Please continue to make meal choices for your child before 8.00am on the day you
wish the booking to be taken.

## Changes in pick up

Please contact the school before 2.30pm by telephone, email at
<aytonps@scotborders.gov.uk> or EduSpot, if you are changing your child’s
pick-up arrangements at the end of the day. It is very important that we know of
changes so that class teachers can be informed.

## Water bottle and Snacks

Please ensure your child is sent to school with a filled water bottle and
a snack for break time, pupils also benefit from “fruit fix” in the
afternoons. As the weather is getting better, please feel free to provide your
child with sun cream.

## Breakfast Club

Breakfast continues to be operated daily. Breakfast club is open from
08.15-08.30am every day and costs 50p. If you are using this service, please
ensure your child arrives before 8.20am. Breakfast Club is a payment item on
ParentPay. Please pay in advance.

## Uniform

Your cooperation with your child wearing [school uniform](/uniform) is greatly
appreciated. We do not encourage black jeans and brightly coloured trainers to
be worn. We would like children to wear black school shoes.
