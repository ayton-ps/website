Dear Parents/Carers,

We have had a busy first couple of weeks of the new term since the October half-term break. The
children have all settled well into their school routines and we look forward to sharing more
examples of learning and updates through Showbie, School Facebook page and in our newsletters. Many
thanks to everyone who came along to parents consultations before the October break. If you were
unable to make or keep an appointment, please do contact your child’s teacher to rearrange via the
school office.

I look forward to working with you all this term. Thank you for your ongoing continued support.

Kind regards,  
Mrs Horsburgh  
Head Teacher

## Message from the PFA

### 🎄 Christmas Raffle Appeal 🎄

It is that time of year again, and the PFA is officially preparing for our annual Christmas Raffle!
In previous years, we have been incredibly grateful for your generous donations of gifts and
vouchers.

This year, we are also aiming to build one or two fantastic food and drink hampers. If you are out
doing your shopping over the coming weeks, please consider picking up an extra bottle of wine, a box
of chocolates, or a festive treat to contribute.

Additionally, if you know of any local businesses that might be willing to support us with a voucher
or gift donation, we would be absolutely thrilled.

**Where to drop off donations:** Please hand any hamper items, gifts, or vouchers to Mrs McAskill at
the school office or directly to any PFA council member.

**Raffle Tickets:** Tickets will be available to purchase from the school office or at the upcoming
nativity performances. If you would like to help us maximize our fundraising by taking a bundle of
tickets away to sell to family, friends, or colleagues, please collect them from the school office.

Thank you so much for your continued support!

### A Huge Thank You!

Finally, a massive thank you to Louise Birkett and all of our wonderful parent volunteers. Your hard
work made the Halloween disco a fantastic success for the children.

We cannot run these events without parent help, if you've not already joined our
[parents WhatsApp](/pfa/wa) please do so to stay in touch for future events.

## ELC and Primary 1 Enrolment Week

**Monday 16th November – Friday 20th November - Session 2027/2028**

Enrolment week for **ELC**, **Primary 1**, **Placement Requests** and **deferred entry to Primary
1**. Communication is being published by Scottish Borders Council and will be distributed to
parents/carers.

The link for enrolment and further information is:
[School and nursery places – Scottish Borders Council](https://www.scotborders.gov.uk/school-nursery-places).
_Please do not apply for a place in the 2027/2028 session before enrolment week or the application
will be rejected_.

If you wish to visit our school or know someone who has a child ready to start school for next
session, please encourage them to contact the school office on 018907 81350 or email the school to
arrange a visit. Thank you.

## P1 and P7 Dental Screening
