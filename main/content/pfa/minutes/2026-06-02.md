### Current term - everything funded?

School trip to Northumberland zoo agreed to be funded in full, tickets and transport, on previous

### Wish lists

We discussed the progress with the previously identified items on the wish list, headphone jacks
have been purchased, there is a salt spreader, we need to check that it’s going to be good enough
for the winter, going to look for donations for lunch clubs - chess boards and crochet needles and
wool.

As the term ends the school plan to do a stock take and identify any additional items for the
sensory room that can be added to next years wish list. We spoke about stylus for use with the
iPads, perhaps that’s something can add to the wish list, DM to look into what kind would be
suitable.

Items identified on a big wish list previously were discussed they are replacement smart boards for
the classrooms, portable stage lights and a new stage. DM to explore budgets for smart boards, it
was agreed we will consider this once playground project is complete.

### Fundraising

We discussed our planned fundraising for the year, next will be the bake sale held on sports day we
will ask for parents to bake and will put on teas and coffees as we have done in previous years. In
September Ayton show book sale fundraiser as done previously, DM suggested the school library is
needing a refresh. Discussed some books going into the sale but also exploring an swap style
arrangement with Barter books in Alnwick.

We spoke about different funding options available for projects such as the smart screens and new
stage.

### School grounds update

JS and DM updated on recent meeting with SBC contractor regarding replacement of bark area. It was
suggested it may be over £6000. Through speaking with other schools in the area DM identified
another option of using SBC work orders from the criminal justice system. This has been carried out
successfully in other schools under SBC supervision and would be a considerable saving.

### Extra maths

ER asked whether there are any additional materials in the class room for maths. DM spoke about the
introduction of magma maths on the iPads and will ask teachers if there is anything else they
require.

### Eyemouth primary school update

JS updated us on her involvement with the amendment to make a stand alone building a priority in the
discussions, the campus model is still an option being explored. A group has been formed to continue
to challenge the process, raise awareness amongst councillors and look to additional funding support
for the school. A letter will be sent from the PFA to the council highlighting theses concerns.
