## Discussion points

### Breakfast club

There are 8–14 children attending breakfast club. There are currently no parent helpers assisting RD
to run breakfast — we have previously had someone daily when there were bigger numbers in
attendance. RD reported that it would be great to have assistance, in particular someone available
from 8:30am to put the room back together and tidy while RD is in the playground with the children.
We have welcomed donations of bread for breakfast club from Darren at the Ayton shop and are
grateful for their continued support. LB suggested exploring the Tesco blue token scheme for
breakfast club funding.

- LB to explore

### Playground updates

Final spending planned for the playground improvement project: priority is for the woodchip area to
be dug out, lined and decked with anti-trip barrier and anti-slip treatment applied. After lots of
previous discussions the council have approved the spending and we are hopeful the work will be
completed in the October break. The work will be done by the SBC work order from the community
justice system. This has been quoted at £5,505.85 and will also include some repairs and painting of
existing wooden furniture.

- DM to book in the work

Remaining funds are to be spent on redoing the playground markings; quotes have already been
obtained.

- DM to let PFA know about costings

LB highlighted the issue of the low-level vegetable beds being used by cats, and suggested reaching
out to local groups such as Abundant Borders or Ayton Garden Society for support.

- LB to follow up

### School needs for the year - transport etc.

We discussed the expected financial support needed by the school from the PFA: buses for the hockey
and rugby festival, dance festival, cross country, Priory Live, and a possible summer 2027 outing to
the Museum of Flight. TF reported on discussion at the Eyemouth High School parents council on the
inequity of funding support to our area for transport, particularly given our geographic location.
TF to share a letter being sent to SBC highlighting our difficulties, and suggested we send a
similar letter. JH explained the new process of transport booking for schools is now centrally
tendered through SBC passenger transport.

- TF to share copy of EHS letter with PFA council

### Wish list from school

DM reported that school is still looking for nurture bits and sensory support items. DM explained
the work the current P6/7 class are doing with alternative seating, to great success.

- DM to request specific items to support

DM reported the school will have a visiting music teacher, John Jacobs, doing music theory with P6/7
initially, with more individual music lessons possibly becoming available soon. The school would
benefit from some new instruments, perhaps some percussion or boomwhackers. JH will look to school
funds to support this initially.

- DM to review current stock

Still to purchase new stylus for iPad use. Will wait to hear about Generation Science again coming
to the school in the new year.

Big project wish list items are a new stage, stage lights, and updating classroom smart screens —
agreed we will pursue these once the playground project is complete.

### School update

All new P1s and returning students have settled well into the new year. A new family have joined the
school, taking the roll up to 65. The three class teachers have all sent out curriculum overviews
for the term to parents. The P6/7 class will be going to Ayton Castle for outdoor education this
term, and with Tweed Forum they will be doing some river dipping — Ayton Castle have requested to
film them using the ground as part of Alan Carr’s TV show. Permission slips have been sent to P6/7
parents, and all children can attend even if they do not want to be involved in filming. There is a
school open afternoon on Tuesday 22 September, and parents’ evenings will be in October. JH reported
that the school improvement plan has been submitted to SBC and will be published soon.
