### Where the money came from

- **Christmas raffle: £643.00**, almost double last year's £328.00.
- **Playground fund: £1,055.00**, from the sponsored walk.
- **Events:** Sports Day raised £286.71 and the book stall at Ayton Flower Show raised £188.60.
- **Other:** Easy Fundraising (£40.17), donations (£31.58), and £40.00 compensation from Bank of
  Scotland after the complaint about the account closure.

### How it was spent

- **Transport: £1,110.64** from the Transport fund, covering the rugby bus, dance festival transport
  and a £800.00 contribution to trip transport. This uses almost all of the money raised by the 2025
  sponsored walk.
- **School trips: £711.75** towards the cost of trips.
- **Christmas: £241.94**
- **Generation Science: £190.00**
- **P7 leavers' gifts: £48.40** for calculators.
- **Administration: £8.50** in Bank of Scotland charges before the account closed.

### Other matters

- **Banking.** We moved to NatWest and closed the Bank of Scotland account.
- **Since the year end.** The balance is £8,166.73 as of 27 October 2026, after the 2026 Flower Show
  stall (£143.00), online book sales (£26.87), less iPad styluses (£46.81).
- **50/50 draw.** The draw has ended. Three 2024 prizes (£41.00), set aside for the winners when
  drawn, were handed over by cheque in September 2025. A £24.00 advance payment was refunded to a
  member.
- **Cow Club.** Ayton & District Cow Club closed and split their remaining funds between three local
  orgs. We have a cheque for £214.61, being paid in on Friday 30 October.
- **Future expenses:**
  - Decking and fencing should be £5,505.85
  - £105.56 for netball tournament transport
  - £143.60 for rugby & hockey festival transport
  - Cheque for £171.43 for last year's Priory Live transport has been given to school but not paid

  - Further £51 for styluses
