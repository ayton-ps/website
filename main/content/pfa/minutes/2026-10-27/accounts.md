## Notes

1. **Basis of preparation.** The accounts are prepared on a receipts and payments basis, using the
   format OSCR recommends for small Scottish charities, which is common practice for parent
   councils. The PFA is a constituted parent council with the powers given by the Scottish Schools
   (Parental Involvement) Act 2006; it is not a registered charity. The accounts record money
   received and paid during the year. Transfers between the PFA's own bank and cash accounts are not
   shown as receipts or payments.
2. **Funds.**
   - _Unrestricted:_ general funds the PFA may spend on any of its purposes.
   - _Playground (restricted):_ grants and donations given for outdoor play equipment and
     improvements to the playground. The trim trail was bought from this fund in 2024–25.
   - _Transport (restricted):_ money raised by the 2025 sponsored walk for transport to school trips
     and events. The fund was created in June 2025; transport costs of £340.57 paid before then were
     met from unrestricted funds.
3. **50/50 draw prizes.** Each prize was set aside in cash for the winner when it was drawn, so it
   is shown as paid in that year. Three 2024 prizes (May £14.00, June £14.00, August £13.00) were
   set aside but not handed over. The £41.00 was held for the winners at 31 July 2025, was not PFA
   money, and was paid to them by cheque in September 2025. The draw has now ended; a member's
   £24.00 advance payment was refunded in September 2025.
4. **Payments to the school.** £2,205.39 was paid to Ayton Primary School, which arranged the trips,
   transport, Christmas presents and Generation Science visit on the PFA's behalf.
5. **Office bearers and members.** No office bearer or member received payment or expenses for their
   role.
6. **Bank accounts.** The Bank of Scotland account was closed in November 2025 and its balance moved
   to NatWest. Bank of Scotland paid £40.00 compensation following a complaint about the closure.
7. **Cash.** £305.31 was held in cash at the year end, mainly from Sports Day. It should be banked
   promptly.
8. **Amount owed at the year end.** The PFA agreed to pay £171.43 for transport to Priory Live,
   which took place before 31 July 2026. It was unpaid at the year end and is not included in
   payments. A cheque has since been written but not yet cashed. It will be met from the remaining
   Transport fund (£35.36) and unrestricted funds (£136.07), leaving the Transport fund empty and
   £830.01 unrestricted.
